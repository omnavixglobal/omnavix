ℹ️ About SAFARQR

SAFARQR — Know. Pay. Relax. Go.

Global QR & Payment Decision Assistant — a traveller-focused information and navigation layer that helps you understand digital payment options before you pay.

🌍 The traveller problem

When you travel, the payment system may change from country to country. You may need to know whether your payment route is supported, which QR network is involved, where it can be used, what currency you will be charged in and what fees may apply.

🔎 KNOWCan I pay here? Check documented payment availability for the selected route.
📷 IDENTIFYWhat is this QR? Scan and understand the payment information encoded in the QR.
💱 UNDERSTANDWhat will it cost? Review currency, reference FX information and possible charges.

🧭 How SAFARQR works

01 — Home CountryIdentify the country of your payment account or payment route.
02 — DestinationSelect the country where you want to pay.
03 — Payment RouteSee documented UPI/QR network and partner information.
04 — CostUnderstand local currency, reference exchange information and possible charges.
05 — Scan & IdentifyScan a merchant QR and review the information SAFARQR can read.
06 — NavigateContinue to your chosen payment app or provider to complete the transaction.
What SAFARQR is: traveller information, payment-route intelligence, QR identification and navigation.
What SAFARQR is not: a bank, wallet, payment processor or payment network. SAFARQR does not hold funds or authorise transactions. The actual payment is completed through the user's selected regulated payment app or provider.

🚀 Beyond payments

The platform can also support traveller information such as destinations, currency information, connectivity, transport and travel preparation. The long-term direction is a broader Global Digital Travel Companion, while keeping payment execution with regulated providers.

🛡️ SAFARQR Safety

SAFARQR does not authorise or execute payments. It provides information and navigation. Always complete the final verification inside your actual payment app or provider.

Before you pay

1. Confirm the payeeCheck that the final merchant/payee name shown by your payment app matches the person or business you intend to pay.
2. Confirm the amountReview the local amount, currency and final amount requested before authorising.
3. Review the exchange rateCompare the displayed exchange information with the rate and conversion details provided by your payment provider.
4. Check chargesLook for provider fees, FX markup, conversion charges or other applicable charges.
5. Verify the QR contextA QR can contain payment information, but scanning it alone does not prove that the merchant owns or controls the destination account.
6. Use official sourcesVisa, entry, payment-network and provider requirements can change. Use the relevant official authority or provider as the final source of truth.

🔐 What SAFARQR can and cannot verify

✓ Can identifyEncoded QR/payment information such as scheme, payee information, amount or reference when available.
⚠ Cannot proveMerchant ownership or that a QR is safe simply because it can be read.
🔒 Final checkThe payment app/provider's final payee and amount screen is the last check before authorisation.
Simple rule: Scan → Understand → Verify → Pay.

How this prototype works

  1. The website loads data/SAFARQR_Global_Corridor_Database_V1.xlsx.
  2. JavaScript reads the Corridor Database worksheet in the browser.
  3. Country/payment information is displayed dynamically.

  5. You can update the Excel corridor records and replace the database file in the website folder; the protected registry remains as a safety floor.
Production recommendation: move the master data to a controlled database/API after the research prototype. Excel is excellent for the first MVP, but it should not remain the production system of record.
